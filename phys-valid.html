Governance Before Assumption

Conditions must be declared before conclusions are granted.

In high-consequence operational, engineering, and capital asset settings, failures of governance rarely happen because formulas are incorrect. They happen because operating conditions drift, boundary constraints are left implicit, or assumptions are treated as facts.

Phys-Valid establishes a disciplined screening layer that verifies whether declared operational parameters satisfy required constraint envelopes before decision packages are submitted for engineering review.

Platform Principles & Governance

Phys-Valid does not replace professional engineering judgment.

It does not predict equipment failure or calculate remaining life.

It enforces explicit constraint validation, evidence admissibility, and complete audit traceability.

Licensed engineers and accountable operational authorities remain responsible for all consequential decisions.

Engineering Governance & Standards Notice

Phys-Valid is a deterministic screening and decision governance tool. It does not replace applicable industrial codes, API standards, ASME requirements, or statutory engineering certifications. It provides structured validation of declared inputs against established mathematical and operational criteria, ensuring that anomalies are flagged and routed through proper engineering review workflows.

Screening Architecture

Five stages of deterministic validation

A transparent progression from input declaration to verified governance trail.

01

Declare Conditions

Operational data, design envelopes, temperature/pressure parameters, and inspection records are explicitly declared.

02

Admissibility Check

Verification of data completeness, measurement lineage, valid sensor calibration, and required evidence provenance.

03

Constraint Screening

Deterministic mathematical evaluation of operating conditions against strict structural and operational boundary limits.

04

Escalation Routing

Any parameter violation, ambiguity, or boundary threshold automatically routes to designated engineering authorities.

05

Traceable Audit Log

An immutable, time-stamped validation record is generated, capturing all rules, inputs, reviews, and sign-offs.
